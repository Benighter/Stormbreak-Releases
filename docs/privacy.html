BENNET / STORMBREAK

Privacy policy

Effective 8 September 2026. This policy covers Stormbreak's Google Play edition, developed by Bennet Nkolele.

Your game stays on your device

Stormbreak does not require an account. Game progress, settings, achievements and score records are stored locally on your device. Its scoreboards are local records, not online profiles.

The Google Play edition does not contain advertising, in-app purchases, developer-operated analytics or a developer-operated account service. It does not collect or send your gameplay data, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier to Bennet.

Google Play and device services

Downloads and updates are managed by Google Play. Google and your device platform may process installation, payment-account, security, diagnostic or device-backup information under their own settings and privacy policies. Stormbreak is a free download and does not charge for in-game items. Android backup, if enabled by your device, may back up local app data.

Vibration is used for optional gameplay feedback. You can disable it in the game's settings.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to answer your request. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is not sold or used for advertising. You can ask for its deletion, subject to any legal retention requirements.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary server-access information, such as IP addresses, under its privacy policy. We have not added tracking scripts, advertising or analytics to this page.

Deleting your data

You can remove local game data using Android's app storage controls or by uninstalling the game. This deletes local progress; manage any Android backups separately in your device or Google account settings. There is no Stormbreak account to delete.

Changes and contact

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated. For privacy or support requests, contact bennet.nkolele1998@gmail.com.
